## What the Zoom integration does

Revenue CRM connects to your Zoom account so that meetings and lessons you hold on Zoom are logged in the CRM without manual entry.

- **Meeting log:** after a meeting ends, Revenue CRM records the date, time, duration and topic on the matching customer or student record.
- **Participant matching:** participants' names and email addresses are matched to contacts (Revenue CRM) or students (school edition), so each meeting appears on the right record.
- **Notes and tasks from transcripts:** when a cloud recording has an audio transcript, Revenue CRM reads the transcript text and turns it into meeting notes, a summary and follow-up tasks. Revenue CRM does not download audio or video files.

The integration is available in Revenue CRM (crm.consilegy.com) and in the school edition operated for our school customers.

## Requirements

- A Revenue CRM account (any plan) or a school-edition account.
- A Zoom account. Transcript features need Zoom cloud recording with audio transcripts turned on (available on Zoom paid plans).

## Adding the app

**Revenue CRM**

1. Sign in to Revenue CRM.
2. Open **Settings → Meetings**.
3. Click **Connect** next to Zoom.
4. Sign in to Zoom if asked, review the permissions, and click **Allow**.
5. You return to Revenue CRM and Zoom shows as connected.

**School edition (for instructors)**

1. Sign in to the school CRM.
2. On the home screen, click **Connect my Zoom** (administrators can also use **Integrations → Zoom**).
3. Sign in to Zoom if asked and click **Allow**.

Each user connects their own Zoom account. Revenue CRM only reads data from the accounts that have authorized it.

## Using the app

- Hold your meetings or lessons on Zoom as usual. No extra steps are needed during the meeting.
- Revenue CRM imports your past meetings automatically once a day. In Revenue CRM you can also click **Import now** in **Settings → Meetings** to import right away.
- Each meeting appears on the matching customer or student record. When a transcript is available, notes and tasks are added once Zoom has finished processing the recording.
- If a participant could not be matched, link their Zoom display name to the right record once (school edition: **Integrations → Zoom participant links**). Past meetings are then matched as well.

## Data we access

Within the scopes you approve on Zoom's authorization screen:

- Your Zoom user information (user ID, account ID, email address, user type)
- Your meeting list and past meeting details
- Past meeting participants (name, email address, join and leave time)
- Your cloud recording list and the transcript (VTT) file

How we handle this data is described in our [Privacy Policy](https://consilegy.com/en/privacy-policy/) (section 12.2, Zoom integration). We do not sell Zoom data or use it for advertising, and our AI providers do not use it to train models.

## Removing the app

You can disconnect at any time in either of these ways:

- **In Revenue CRM:** open **Settings → Meetings** and click **Disconnect** next to Zoom.
- **In Zoom:** sign in to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us/), open **Manage → Added Apps**, find **Revenue CRM**, and click **Remove**.

When the app is removed, we immediately delete the Zoom tokens for that account and stop retrieving data from Zoom. Meeting records that were already imported stay in your CRM until you delete them. To have them deleted, contact us at the address below.

### Tagline (one line)
A CRM that doesn't create work. AI does the data entry.

### Short description (~100 words)
Revenue CRM is a CRM for small and mid-sized businesses that connects marketing, sales and customer management on a single customer record, and lets AI do the recording. Snap a business card and it becomes a contact; talk through a meeting and AI transcribes it, extracts the key points and next actions, and creates tasks and contacts from the notes. 175 RevOps automations ship enabled-ready, and each edition (B2B sales, contractors/agencies, SaaS resellers, schools) comes pre-configured for that business, so you never start from an empty CRM. Built in Japan; data stored in Tokyo; UI in Japanese, English and Traditional Chinese.

### Long description (~250 words)
A customer relationship is one continuous flow: marketing creates the first touch, sales takes it over, the deal closes, and the relationship continues after that. In practice the flow breaks at the boundaries between departments, people and tools — and when companies try to reconnect it with MA, SFA and CRM tools, they create new work: data entry for the field, configuration for admins, and operations for the organization. In organizations that cannot dedicate someone to running the CRM, that operational load is what kills adoption.

Revenue CRM was designed the other way around: instead of making people fit the CRM, the CRM fits the way people already work.

- **AI does the recording.** Business cards, handwritten notes and call recordings are captured by AI. Meeting notes become tasks and contacts automatically. AI drafts the next message. The team confirms instead of typing.
- **175 automations, built in.** Lead routing, follow-up reminders, stage-change notifications, approvals, renewal alerts — a diagnostic reads your current setup and suggests up to eight automations to switch on.
- **Pre-configured by business type.** Fields, pipelines and automations are designed per edition: B2B sales, contractors/agencies, SaaS resellers, and schools (B2C). Choose your edition and start with a configured workspace.
- **One customer record across MA, SFA and CRM.** Forms, lists, email campaigns and lead scoring; deals, pipelines and forecasting; health scores, tickets and renewals — all on the same data.

Open by design: REST API, GraphQL, MCP server, webhooks, and read-import from HubSpot and Salesforce. Role-based permissions, audit log, 2FA, Google sign-in. Data stored in Japan (Tokyo). Customer data is contractually excluded from AI model training; you can also bring your own AI API key.

Founded by Seiko Yamaguchi after eight years and 40+ HubSpot and Salesforce implementations.
